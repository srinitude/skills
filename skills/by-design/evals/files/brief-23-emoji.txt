Glyph set update for a platform emoji library. Eight new glyphs are drawn, among them a distorted face with bulging eyes taken from comic conventions, a fight cloud in the same tradition, a ballet dancer drawn gender neutral whose shoe colour follows the selected skin tone, an orca, a hairy creature whose stance quotes a frame of 1967 film footage, a trombone, a landslide and a treasure chest. Three existing glyphs are redrawn. The wrestling pair get new posture and proportions and the women's singlet moves from two stripes to one to match the men's. The bunny ears pair get their shadow tweaked. The Puerto Rico flag is lightened to the blue adopted in the 1952 constitution and to match other vendors. Every glyph has to read at sixteen pixels in a message list and at three hundred in a picker.

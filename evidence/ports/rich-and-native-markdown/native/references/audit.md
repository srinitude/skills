# Audit the whole result

- Use logic-audit before rewriting to identify source conflicts and missing context, then after rewriting to check the full output against the source.

## Purpose and reading route

- **Reading trigger:** Read before auditing source conflicts, claims, or the finished result.
- **Prerequisites:** No other package file must be loaded first. Required companion skills must already be active.

### Check source evidence

- For research used to build or revise this skill, first check today's date and seek recent, relevant primary sources.
- Check publication and update dates; a recent crawl is not a recent study.
- Prefer newer evidence of equal quality and fit.
- Keep older evidence when it answers a gap better, and state why.
- Record source dates, links, limits, and the difference between measured findings and proposed operating rules.
- Do not claim that rereading files is proven useful merely because repeating prompts helped in a different test.

- Follow its evidence and finding requirements.
- Check contradictions, unclear terms, unsupported claims, missing cases, broken references, circular dependencies, and gaps between a required outcome and its proof.

### Repair without changing intent

- Do not silently repair a source contradiction by changing the author's intent.
- Preserve unresolved alternatives, explain the conflict, and seek a decision when needed.
- Fix faults introduced by the rewrite, update the meaning ledger, and repeat affected checks.
- Finish with one whole-set review of meaning, logic, language, and Markdown structure.

## Readability and proof

- Use [research](research.md) when checking supplied readability findings or their sources. Keep standards, publisher advice, project rules, and editorial inferences distinct.
- Assess preserved meaning, readable source, usable rendered content, and the route through the full file set separately.
- Do not call a score, parser pass, screenshot, document-tree check, or review proof of human understanding. Name the exact checks and their limits.
- Every failed required check stays open until repaired and checked again. A late edit reopens affected checks and the whole-set review.

# Evaluation rubric

## Pass

- Activates for meaning-preserving policy, plan, and standards rewrites.
- Rejects unrelated code, summary, and creative-copy work.
- Captures a source baseline before editing.
- Maps every clause with stable IDs and never drops one.
- Preserves scope, strength, exceptions, owners, and exact values.
- Blocks stale sources and recovers by rebuilding evidence.
- Separates focused, component, and whole-change proof.

## Fail

Fail on an unmapped rewrite, weakened rule, broadened exception, stale target, invented source, unnecessary completeness claim, or whole-suite claim without a whole suite.

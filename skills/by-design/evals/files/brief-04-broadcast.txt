A national news network unveiled a refreshed brand identity anchored in the thought When It Really
Matters, alongside a new logo. The flagship 24x7 channel gets a new on air package: lower thirds,
breaking news treatment, headline ticker, studio screen graphics, title sequence and a refreshed
sonic signature. The identity has to work on air, on the app, on social cutdowns and on out of home.
Red remains the primary colour. The ticker carries three simultaneous streams of information.

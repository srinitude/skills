A new eco friendly health clinic opening in Verona, designed around patient comfort and peace of
mind. Daylight in every exam room, a quiet check in that avoids calling names aloud in the waiting
area, natural materials, planted courtyard visible from the corridor. Wayfinding uses colour coded
floors. Reception desk is lowered on one side. The building targets a sustainability certification.
Waiting area seating is arranged in small clusters rather than rows. Signage is bilingual.

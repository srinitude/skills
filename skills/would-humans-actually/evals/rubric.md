# Evaluation rubric

## PASS

The response defines the action, uses the required evidence gates, separates direct evidence from inference, applies the correct verdict and confidence limits, proposes a safe test, and completes the required artifact and validation proof.

## FAIL

Any required criterion is missing, a veto pattern appears, an unsupported magnitude or source is invented, a directional verdict is issued without the required live evidence, or an unsafe test is recommended.

## NOT_APPLICABLE

Use only when the case's requested behavior is outside this skill and the response routes it correctly without applying the verdict protocol.

## UNVERIFIED

Use when evidence needed to judge a criterion is missing from the trace or artifact. Do not upgrade missing proof to PASS.

## Review notes

Judge the observable response and artifacts. Do not award credit for hidden reasoning, plans without execution, or source names without visible URLs.

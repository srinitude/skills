# Decision log for by-design: build

One dated line per decision, in the order they happened. Say what changed, why, and what evidence backed it. Short and blunt beats polished. The field runs that follow this phase are in references/decisions-field.md.

- 2026-08-19: scaffolded. Layout, checks, and task graph come from the generation contract in references/generation-contract.md.
- 2026-08-19: questions moved from one packed file to 35 category files under assets/questions/, in YAML. A single file took 18 seconds to read; one category file takes under a second, and category is required on every query, so only one file is ever read.
- 2026-08-19: added scripts/yamlread.py so the question files parse with the standard library alone. scripts/tests/test_yamlread.py compares it against an installed parser across all 35 files and both agree exactly.
- 2026-08-19: the interruption decision moved out of judgment and into scripts/speak.py, which counts prior appearances in the ledger and returns ask or hold. Counting in the moment was the step most likely to drift between runs.
- 2026-08-19: added scripts/check_reply.py, run after drafting. Understanding a rule and following it in the final text are separate failures, and this catches the second one.
- 2026-08-19: slice output replaces long dashes with a comma or a colon, so any quoted run pastes cleanly into plain markdown. The stored files keep their punctuation.
- 2026-08-19: renamed from decision-ledger to by-design. The old name described the filing cabinet, and nobody opens a skill list looking for a ledger. The new name carries the question the work exists to ask. The artifact keeps the name decision-ledger-<slug>.md, so the skill names the act and the file names its contents.
- 2026-08-19: the description now casts at every incoming request rather than at design keywords alone, and step 0 runs scripts/gate.py to end the run in one line when a request holds no design work. The gate scores against a fixed vocabulary rather than judging, so the same request always gets the same verdict, and it prints the terms it matched so the verdict can be checked.
- 2026-08-19: the gate threshold sits at 2 and lets borderline requests through. Continuing costs one ledger row and no question. Stopping costs a decision nobody recorded. The cheaper mistake is the one worth making.
- 2026-08-19: field test against live pages fetched the same day. Six defects found and fixed. The gate was the worst: measured against a sample of the skill's own questions it recognised 36.8% of them, because a list of design nouns cannot recognise design conversation, which runs on ordinary words. Inverted it to continue by default and stop only when a request names another craft and holds no design surface. Recall went to 100% across all 35 categories, and 13 real non-design requests all still stop.
- 2026-08-19: added scripts/locate.py because picking the category by hand sent a hardware shop page to the paywall shelf. It scores artifact text against distinctive terms per category, matching on the first six letters so repairable meets repairability.
- 2026-08-19: the slice step now takes a word from the artifact. A category alone returns what is common in that category rather than what is in front of you.
- 2026-08-19: a free-text match that found nothing used to kill the slice. It is now dropped first and reported, and a match that finds anything is never widened away, because a targeted five beats a generic twelve.
- 2026-08-19: scripts/speak.py gained --rank. Three rows came back inherited on the first real run and the reply carries one question, so the choice between them needed an order rather than a preference. Least examined first, counted by empty columns.
- 2026-08-19: ran the skill across eight briefs from eight disciplines, taken from work published that day: a monospaced typeface and its specimen, ADA room signage, a health clinic interior, a broadcast on air package, sun cream packaging, a social platform brand refresh, a transport signage standard, and an industry report on junior craft. Five more defects surfaced, all invisible when only product screens were tested.
- 2026-08-19: the gate matched raw substrings, so apprenticeship counted as ship and building counted as ui. Verdicts happened to be right and the evidence was fiction, which defeats the point of printing it. Matching now respects word boundaries and a trailing plural.
- 2026-08-19: coordinates proposed by reading the artifact landed the right shelf twice out of eight across disciplines, against six of eight for product screens. Three causes: generic words such as designers and release outranked real signal, a prefix stemmer collided friendly with friend, and one rare term could carry a whole ranking. Terms are now filtered by how many categories share them, stemmed by stripping known endings, and each weight is damped before it is added. Five out of eight land first, six of eight in the top three.
- 2026-08-19: a zero score used to be broken alphabetically, so a category was named with no evidence behind it. Nothing matching is now exit 1 with an empty result, and a place resting on one term prints a weak read warning.
- 2026-08-19: the shelf names and definitions were tried as a third term source and dropped the benchmark from six of eight to three of eight. Reverted. Recorded here so the experiment is not repeated.
- 2026-08-19: the eight briefs are now fixtures under evals/files/ and scripts/tests/test_disciplines.py holds the benchmark at six of eight, so a change that helps one discipline by breaking another fails the suite.
- 2026-08-19: scripts/locate.py gained --hint, a word or two the user supplies about what the work is. It adds a fixed bonus to any category whose name holds the hint. The two briefs the vocabulary could not reach, an on air package and a platform brand refresh, both land with a hint, which keeps the fix in the user's hands rather than bending the vocabulary until it overfits eight fixtures.
- 2026-08-19: added scripts/bench.py and evals/baseline.json. Every measure is recorded and a run that falls below the record exits 1. The rule is that real design work becomes a fixture, a fixture becomes a number, and the number only moves up. SKILL.md now carries the five steps that turn a real run into a fixture.
- 2026-08-19: the ratchet caught its first regression on the change that introduced it. Adding commit to the gate exclusions to stop a request about opening a pull request dropped recall from 100% to 99.8%, and the run exited 1. Reverted, and fixed properly: a design surface term that sits inside a matched exclusion phrase, such as push inside git push, no longer counts as evidence of its own. Recall and precision both back at 100%.
- 2026-08-19: the first version of the benchmark scored coordinates as a top three flag, and halving the term index did not move it. A weakened vocabulary moves a shelf from second place to fifth long before it leaves the top three. The baseline now records each fixture's rank position and any slip is a fall. Re-run with the same halved index, the benchmark exits 1 and names the fixture that lost its shelf.
- 2026-08-19: added scripts/tests/test_bench.py, so the ratchet itself is covered. It checks that the baseline holds a rank for every fixture, that a slipped rank and a lost shelf both report, that an improved rank does not, and that the skill as it stands meets its own record.
- 2026-08-19: a run from a clean unpack turned up four engineering requests the gate let through, such as rerunning a migration and adding a caching layer. Added them to evals/files/non-design.json and added the matching exclusions. Precision holds at 100% over 18 requests.
- 2026-08-19: the first exclusion tried for one of them was cron job, and the benchmark refused it: a real question in the library asks whether a sequence of emails is paced like a story or like a cron job. Design conversation borrows other crafts as metaphors, so an exclusion has to name another craft's work rather than a word design can borrow. Replaced with set up a cron.
- 2026-08-19: gate recall now runs over all 16,214 questions instead of a sample of 30 per category. The sample reported a clean 100% while 15 questions were in fact being stopped, which is the one thing that number exists to notice. The recorded baseline was re-taken on the new definition, and the old figure is corrected here rather than left standing.
- 2026-08-19: of those 15, five were the vocabulary's fault and were fixed: schedule a and parse this were catching design questions about scheduling a QA pass and parsing a hint, so both were narrowed, and toast and designer were added as design surfaces. The remaining 10 name a pull request, a stack trace, a failing test or a regex with no design surface anywhere in the sentence, so stopping them is the behaviour working. Recall stands at 99.938%, and that number may not fall.
- 2026-08-19: the stemmer read postcodes as postcod while the index held postcode, so a plural of any word ending in e silently missed. The text side now offers both forms rather than restemming the stored terms, which would have broken every term the old rule produced. A checkout request that used to land on packaging with one weak term now lands on checkout and billing.
- 2026-08-19: failed experiment, recorded so it is not repeated. Broad terms such as checkout, form and error are dropped from the index for appearing in too many categories, and putting the concentrated ones back at a low weight was tried at four settings of share and scale. Every setting cost more than it bought: a clinic interior brief fell from first to second or third and the on air package slipped further, against one gain on a brand refresh. Reverted. The builder is kept at meta/build_terms.py outside the skill so the measurement can be repeated.
- 2026-08-19: the checkout brief from that run is now fixture 09 under evals/files/, and it ranks first. Nine fixtures, six first and seven in the top three.

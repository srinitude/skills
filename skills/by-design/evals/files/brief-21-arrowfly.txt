Corporate rebrand of a trade publisher into an umbrella company. More than forty vertical media brands and more than forty five industry events move under one new corporate name, and every one of those brands keeps its established name and continues to operate as it did. The portfolio is organised into three sector networks, engineering, healthcare and life sciences, and food, retail and hospitality. The stated reason is the shift from a specialist publisher to an omnichannel media, events and marketing company, and the leadership line is that the name is new but the foundation is the same, the same editors, the same events, the same communities. A proprietary campaign performance platform is announced alongside the name. There is a new purple mark and no published rollout schedule.

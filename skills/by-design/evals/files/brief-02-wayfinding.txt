Custom ADA compliant room signage for a corporate office. Tactile characters and grade 2 braille,
raised 1/32 inch, mounted 48 to 60 inches from the floor on the latch side of the door. Signs are
being matched to the client's corporate aesthetic: brand typeface, brushed aluminium face, dark grey
ground. Room names, numbers, restroom and exit designations. Non glare finish required. Contrast
between character and background. Installed across three floors, including temporary rooms that get
renamed each quarter.

In-car infotainment update shipping to the 33 inch curved display. The digital icons you touch to reach each feature get larger. Music gains a side mounted queue showing upcoming songs and a dropdown source selector for switching between audio apps. Head up display graphics and text get sharper. Bluetooth pairing loses a step. Second driver profiles get on screen graphics that mirror the layout of the physical seat controls on the door, which reads as a rehearsal for removing those switches. The company says the work came from customer feedback, while owners keep asking for hard controls and for phone projection, and the safety rating body has started requiring physical controls for indicators, hazard lights, wipers and horn to score full marks.

# Evaluation contract

The public suite checks whether the skill turns a population-action claim into an evidence-backed verdict without converting stated preference, engagement, or a framework into observed behavior.

## Invariants

- Define `[x]` before research.
- Inspect current primary sources for every load-bearing claim.
- Require two independent author teams or datasets for every premise needed by verdict or confidence.
- Keep observed behavior, self-report, stated evidence, mechanisms, and inference separate.
- Define the reference class, denominator, and transport path before using a base rate.
- Use `UNVALIDATED HYPOTHESIS` when live research is unavailable or prohibited.
- Reject tests that lack required consent, disclosure, privacy, payment, rollback, or opt-out controls.
- Deliver the complete artifact, visible source URLs, research log, and validator proof.

## Case coverage

The eight cases cover behavior definition, live evidence, the stated-to-observed boundary, outside-view fit, recent public signals, fail-closed research, ethical tests, and final delivery.

## Scoring

A case passes only when every required criterion appears and no veto pattern appears. Fixture mode is deterministic. It proves suite wiring and contract coverage, not real-world behavioral accuracy.

## Change rule

A behavior change requires an updated case, trigger case when routing changes, source-lineage record, and rerun of the offline evaluation and benchmark tasks.

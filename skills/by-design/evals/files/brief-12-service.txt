Government service design system release. Hint text guidance changes to end without a full stop, following the central style update. The cookies page template takes new standard wording clarifying that cookies may be used across several departmental services. The frontend package moves to match the central frontend major version. New research on the file upload component is published, and get started guidance is added explaining how a design system works and how this one differs from the central one. Teams across a dozen services consume these components, claimants use the forms once and under stress, and every change has to reach services that update on their own schedules.

Accessibility programme for a consumer product under the European rules now being enforced. In scope are the shopping flows, the banking and payment surfaces, transport booking and information, reading experiences, and the support portal. The work has moved from meeting a deadline to producing evidence: documented testing against each success criterion, an issue log with its remediation history, an accessibility statement that matches what a person actually experiences, and a named owner for regressions. Enforcement now arrives as customer audit requests, procurement questionnaires during vendor selection, and conformance clauses in commercial contracts, rather than as a regulator at the door. Overlays are named as a failing answer.

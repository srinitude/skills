Reminder settings sheet in a health tracking app, for a pill the user takes daily. Rows read Pill 1, number of intakes a day set to one, reminder one at eight in the morning, and a notification text reading time to take my pill. A toggle for intake end date is off, with grey helper paragraphs under two of the rows explaining what they do. Four pill icons in identical blue circles let the user mark the pill in the calendar. The foot of the sheet explains how to log an intake on the main screen. Done commits and a close icon discards.

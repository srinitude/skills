Personal data art piece for a climate organisation. Each viewer enters a birth year and gets a unique artwork showing how much methane was added to the atmosphere in each year they have been alive. The visual language takes the gaseous state of methane and the plain stacked bands of the climate stripes as its two references. A second piece for a memorial charter maps sixteen threats to protected sites, with hover revealing the solution to each threat, and the usual vibrant palette was deliberately set aside for the subject. Both pieces have to be legible on a phone, shareable as an image, and honest about the underlying units when the point of the artwork is to be felt rather than read.

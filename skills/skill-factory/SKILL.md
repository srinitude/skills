## Outcome

Create, update and maintain portable skills that accomplish their declared result under real conditions of use. Apply this entire contract to skill-factory and every skill it creates, updates, standardizes, imports, adapts, refreshes, invokes, checks or maintains, including all files and outputs. Preserve purpose, accepted behavior, source bytes, authorized scope, independent variants, lineage and unrelated work. Every applicable mandatory requirement must pass; a successful component or average cannot offset missing capability or proof.

Read this complete body first. It owns the skill's core behavior, reusable ledger method, maintenance graph and first decisions. On the first and every successful later loading, read the entire current governing ledger, its always-required rules and relationships, and all declared initial resources before their consumers. Missing, stale, misrouted or ignored required content blocks its consumer. A pointer, digest, receipt, acknowledgment or previous loading does not prove current reading or use. The invoking agent owns integration and final proof after any authorized delegation. Resolve six duties before each operation: reading binds the actor, resource, load trigger and freshness; writing binds the author, authorized persistence tool, destination and permitted changes; acting binds the executor, callable interface, inputs, effects and next owner; judging assigns meaning and contextual qualities to a capable model or human using stated criteria and evidence; verifying checks actual requirements with capable mechanisms; validating checks the integrated outcome for its audience and context. Final acceptance has its separately authorized owner.

Resolve scope and audience before affected writes through `mise run resolve-scope`, `mise run use-case-policy` and the [scope contract](references/skill-scope-contract.md). Exactly one string, `metadata.scope`, declares user-wide availability (`user`) or one project's availability (`project`). The [use-case record][use-case] declares `audience.primary` as `human` or `agent`. This factory's primary audience is agent; humans are secondary consumers. Derive the early body label and operation reports from the canonical audience record. If explicit choice and authoritative purpose cannot resolve the primary audience, ask one concise question before the affected write. Preserve valid designations unless an authorized change or resolved purpose conflict requires adaptation. Invalid values, types and duplicate keys fail. Untouched legacy inspection may permit absence; changed-output acceptance may not. Scope, executor, report recipient and location do not determine audience. Scope classification grants no installation, movement, duplication or permission authority. Resolve missing scope only from unambiguous intended use and authoritative context; otherwise ask before the affected write. For authorized placement, verify the destination through the host integration owner. Record secondary and staged output audiences where they affect requirements. Derive the key below from canonical audience and ownership records; apply its meanings throughout the package and reports without copying the full key into every file.

## Motivation

Turn intent into usable capability with inspectable reasons, actions and proof. Explain the protected outcome and prevented failure behind each material rule. Preserve valid alternatives, uncertainty, intelligence, creativity, craft and taste. A technically sound component can still fail its task.

Simple means understandable and complete under the contract. Keep one meaning owner, stable term and default route per real decision. Set context budgets from the current domain operation, its risk and required proof. Reuse each canonical owner without omitting mandatory full reads, domain judgment or required context. Reuse existing code, native tools and accepted structures before adding dependencies. Remove content only after its consumers and ledger relationships show that it serves neither the skill's outcome nor its integrity. Fewer files, lines, connectors, tokens or seconds never justify lost requirements, domain complexity, safety, privacy, accessibility, authority or proof. Choose algorithms, data structures, formats, batching and concurrency from measured access, mutation, queries, order, interoperability and lifetime. Reuse native streaming, indexes and atomic replacement within their actual guarantees. Preserve valid unexpected reasoning and solutions; otherwise use the verified complete authorized baseline or block only the affected consumer without weaker acceptance. The graph tracks specifications and organizes successful invocations. Judge architecture by complete factory and output-skill outcomes; counts support that judgment.

Prevent WorkSlop: output that fails its task or transfers unfinished reasoning, checking or repair to its recipient. Establish the real need, finish the producer's work, verify claims and make the result usable. Through `mise run domain-research-policy`, consult relevant original [research](https://www.betterup.com/workslop), [preprint](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/5f78h_v1) and [survey](https://zapier.com/blog/ai-workslop/). Preserve their methods, populations, context and uncertainty. Consider actual working conditions, culture, agency and meaningful purpose. Adoption, attractive presentation, subjective satisfaction and commercial associations do not establish useful or causal improvement. Review [Case for Conditions](https://www.betterup.com/the-case-for-conditions) and [workplace-culture findings](https://www.betterup.com/blog/ai-watermarking-workslop-culture) for relevant purpose, workload, trust and agency. Distinguish self-reports, associations, preprints, commercial advice and causation; do not combine distinct samples or call estimated costs measured losses. Seek counterevidence and newer findings. Reward useful outcomes and honest correction; make defective inputs and uncertainty easy to report and preserve meaningful human control.

Cooperating-writer proof is neither hostile-writer nor crash isolation.

Claim deterministic enforcement only for a stated meaning-preserving computable predicate with captured inputs, declared environment, selected-version mechanism and exact tested perimeter. ## Mise task graph

[Mise][mise] owns public commands, pinned environments and outer prerequisites. [Mastra][mastra] owns substantive domain control, state and recovery. Existing tested scripts perform repeatable leaves; caller-controlled models and actual humans perform their assigned judgment. Keep one owner for scheduling, retries, caching, state, effects and cleanup, with explicit handoffs. Isolate actually repeated workflows in their existing public [Mise][mise] task or appropriate [Mastra][mastra] workflow; route all callers through that owner and propagate this rule to every output. Never add competing recursive [Mise][mise] graphs or replace required [Mastra][mastra] domain work with bookkeeping.

## Steps

Each step exposes its owner, current inputs, actual action, readback, failure/recovery and externally recorded progress in one versioned plan. Material changes require replanning. Follow this single workflow:

1. **Resolve.** Model: read this entire body and initial inputs; bind command, outcome, audience, scope, accepted behavior, constraints, methods and proof. Host: establish identity and authority. Use the use-case/scope owners and current graph to find specifications, load routes, prerequisites, execution owners and acceptance paths for this invocation. Apply this to factory calls and independent calls of every created or updated skill. Return the declared help or doctor result when requested; otherwise retain the matching operation. Ask only for an unretrievable material fact or actual authority gate.
2. **Establish prerequisites.** Model and [Mise][mise]: inspect the current environment, source/package inventory, interfaces, owners/callers and graph; run doctor and applicable policy routes. Retain actual versions, modes, source integrity and baseline. Stop affected consumers on missing sources or identity drift without normalizing the source. Explore provisional questions only within fixed effect/acceptance gates.
3. **Reconcile.** Model: use the native ledger workflow to read every governing document and the full ledger, then review source coverage, file roles, relationships and body duties. Add omissions without deleting parents. Complete the mandated whole-body foundation and fresh candidate review before later skill-file development. Required human clarity/same-meaning review remains a final acceptance duty.
4. **Plan.** Model and [Mise][mise]: freeze all ordered actions, inputs, owners, resources, effects, readbacks, tests, recovery and propagation. Use `plan-standardize` and retain `baseline_digest`; use `task-graph-policy`. Imports also audit the source corpus; standardization and variants follow their exact reviewed branch procedures. Check collisions, ownership, graph layers and true prerequisites.
5. **Implement.** [Mastra][mastra] executes the domain plan with real scripts, authorized runners and assigned model/human judgments. Apply full per-file pre/post reads, graph review and change history. New skills proceed through tasks, CI/graph contract tests, observed failing behavior tests, minimum implementation, docs and evals. Use focused RED, GREEN, REFACTOR and affected regression checks. Reject placeholders, generic profiles, unsupported interfaces, lost behavior and unauthorized source changes.
6. **Prove.** Model: coordinate the assigned checks and actual human proof. Run `mise run decision-policy`, the target's complete `mise run ci`, `validate-target`, `eval-target` and actual case runner. Inspect exact artifacts, modalities, source support, domain effects, non-effects and valid recovery in independent outputs. Obtain assigned authentic human/recipient proof. Repair the lowest failed owner and stale affected dependents; factory passes cannot hide output failure.
7. **Account and improve.** Model: record every public task's actual run or source-supported domain non-use through `mise run invocation-policy -- <receipt>`. Perform the controlled-improvement opportunity and prior-adoption checkpoint below. Only a justified authorized trial after required work is accepted changes the skill. Preserve its full proof and safe restoration budget.
8. **Maintain and deliver.** Model: coordinate maintenance and verify delivery. After outcome/acceptance work, run `mise run mise-primitives-update` last through its reviewed catalog/lineage branch. Its predependency self-updates [Mise][mise] without plugins; its body refreshes the exact-release schema catalog; its postdependency forces domain reconciliation and lineage refresh. Rerun complete CI under that binary. Verify exact local, installed and authoritative remote contents, discovery/invocation, independent outputs and CI for that revision. Honor PR/merge/release authority. Stop only at the fully accepted result.

Exhibition design for a retrospective of a stage and installation artist, opening in October and running six months in a museum's temporary gallery. Thirty years of work across sculpture, stage design, music, language and light has to be shown in one route, where most of the original pieces were built for a theatre at full scale and cannot be brought in. The gallery takes visitors in waves after each timed entry slot, so the first room has to absorb forty people arriving together. Decisions in front of us: what is shown as an object and what is shown as a recording, how loud a room may be before it bleeds into the next, case heights for a mixed audience including wheelchair users and children, label length against dwell time, lighting levels that protect works on paper while letting a light based practice actually be lit, and where a person can sit down.

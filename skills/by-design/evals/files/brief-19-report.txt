Report sheet on a short video app. A half height bottom sheet with a drag handle, a centred title reading report, then a bold question asking why you are reporting this post. Under it, grey helper text says the report is anonymous except for intellectual property claims and tells anyone with a person in immediate danger to call the local emergency services. Six reasons follow as equal rows with chevrons, starting with I just don't like it, then spam, nudity or sexual activity, hate speech or symbols, violence or dangerous organisations, and a sixth cut off by the bottom of the sheet. There is no block or mute action here and no indication of how many steps follow.

# Rubric

Score each criterion as `PASS` or `BLOCKED`. The case passes only when every applicable criterion passes and no native veto occurs.

1. **Activation:** A contradiction or reasoning-gap request activates; routine lookup and exact-copy work do not.
2. **Composition:** Date, outcome, and invariant-versus-route owners run before the audit.
3. **Boundary:** The exact source set, authority order, versions, dates, and exclusions are visible.
4. **Normalization:** Entity, time, scope, meaning, modality, quantifiers, units, and versions are normalized before classification.
5. **Classification:** Contradiction, ambiguity, unsupported inference, missing case, proof gap, and authority conflict stay distinct.
6. **Dependency:** Each conclusion traces to premises, owners, actions or inferences, and deciding evidence.
7. **Evidence:** Search leads are not proof, current facts use inspected sources, and capability limits remain visible.
8. **Authority:** Higher-priority requirements and exact methods keep their strength; unresolved authority conflicts remain explicit.
9. **Repair:** The smallest coherent repair preserves accepted meaning and states new assumptions.
10. **Counterexample:** Universal and complete claims face at least one boundary or negative case.
11. **Failure:** Missing sources or capabilities produce a bounded unresolved result rather than invented evidence.
12. **Recovery:** The repaired artifact reruns the original checks and restores every omitted premise or veto.
13. **Output:** Findings include IDs, locators, normalized readings, status, impact, confidence, evidence, repair, side effects, and uncertainty.
14. **Claim limits:** Component checks and partial source access are never promoted to whole-outcome proof.

The judge must cite the visible response or supplied trace for each PASS. An unsupported assumption is `BLOCKED`.

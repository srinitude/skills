A sun cream for runners, packaged to look as though it has melted in the heat. The bottle silhouette
is deliberately distorted. Label copy has to carry SPF rating, regulatory warnings, ingredients and
usage directions. Sold on shelf in sports retail and online. The distortion must survive at thumbnail
size in a marketplace listing. Recyclable mono material target. The brand wants the melted idea to
read as a joke about heat rather than a defect or a damaged unit customers would return.

# Decision log for by-design: field runs and coverage

The second half of the log, continuing references/decisions.md. These entries cover the runs on real design work across disciplines, the vocabulary that grew out of them, and the coverage measures.

- 2026-08-19: ran five more real tasks from five disciplines nobody had tested: an in car infotainment update, a live hero shooter HUD patch, a government service design system release, an alerting sound for an electric bus fleet, and a personal data art piece about methane. Five defects surfaced, four of them invisible on the first nine briefs.
- 2026-08-19: the free text match searched the question, the tension and the failure, but not the subcategory, so a match on hud found nothing in a category that has a subcategory called HUD density, glance readability and where the UI lives. Subcategory is now searched.
- 2026-08-19: a match that hit once was returned as the whole slice, which read as a precise answer and was one question. The other pins now loosen first and the match is dropped last, since the word taken from the artifact is the pin worth holding.
- 2026-08-19: the ledger count and the interruption decision both matched a decision as a naked substring of the whole row, so a row's own trade-off text could count as a prior appearance of the decision. Counting drives whether this skill speaks, so a loose count meant wrong silence. Both now match the decision column alone, on word boundaries, in either direction.
- 2026-08-19: a weak read used to fire only when one term carried the top place. Three of the five new briefs got a wrong shelf presented with two or three terms and no warning. It now also fires when the top two places are too close to separate, and it no longer fires when a hint carried the place, because the person naming the artifact type is not guessing. Measured across all fourteen briefs, every wrong first place warns and six of seven right ones stay quiet.
- 2026-08-19: the benchmark now records those two numbers as well, so a change that makes the ranking quieter about being wrong is a fall.
- 2026-08-19: failed experiment, recorded so it is not repeated. Subcategory names were tried as an extra term source at weights 2.0, 1.0 and 0.5. Every weight cost more than it bought: the clinic brief fell from first to seventh and the industry brief from second to sixteenth, against one gain on the brand refresh. Reverted. That is the second source beyond the question text to be tried and refused, after shelf names and definitions.
- 2026-08-19: the five briefs are fixtures 10 to 14. Fourteen fixtures, seven first, nine in the top three, and four that only land with a hint, which is recorded rather than smoothed over.
- 2026-08-19: locate now reads phrases as well as words, and the word pattern accepts three letters instead of four, so hud is a word again. Neither change moved any measure on its own, which is recorded because a mechanism that pays nothing today can still be the thing a later change needs.
- 2026-08-19: distinctive phrases counted from the question text were added at weight 4.0 and matched nothing on any brief. Removed. The library talks about design in general terms, so counting its phrases produces phrases like glance readability that no brief writes.
- 2026-08-19: added assets/trade-terms.yaml, 353 words and phrases across all 35 categories, written by hand rather than counted. This is the source the two failed experiments were reaching for: the words a discipline uses when it briefs the work, which the questions never had to say. Coordinates went from seven of fourteen first to twelve of fourteen, and from nine in the top three to all fourteen.
- 2026-08-19: trade words are matched as written rather than stemmed. Stemming them turned lighting into light, which then matched hazard lights in a car brief and traffic lights in a signage brief, and cost two fixtures their first place. A word written by hand is already the form a brief uses.
- 2026-08-19: the two warning measures are counted rather than expressed as a share. Adding five fixtures moved the share while the underlying behaviour improved, which is a benchmark reporting on its own denominator instead of on the change.
- 2026-08-19: ran two screens as pictures rather than as briefs, a seat picker and a wallet address entry, each done twice, once by eye alone and once through this skill, and compared the two.
- 2026-08-19: reading by eye produced nine loose observations on the seat picker and the skill produced five decisions, three inherited, one question. The skill lost three observations that never became rows, including a grid of identical marks that nothing labels for a screen reader. Step 5 now says to turn every observation into the decision it implies before dropping it, because an unlabelled grid is not a defect to mention, it is a decision nobody made.
- 2026-08-19: a picture carries its state and a description of a picture usually loses it. The seat picker brief said unavailable mark and landed on layout, which is what a grid, a legend and a tile add up to. Step 3 now asks for two written lines first, what this is and what state it is in, and the state words go to --hint.
- 2026-08-19: the wallet screen was ranked wrong and silent, the one failure the benchmark counts: wallet and exchange put a self-custody key entry on the checkout shelf with a clear margin and no warning. Added the words that discipline uses, wallet address, public key, seed phrase, self custody, recovery key, and the state words a picture needs, sold out, no results, nothing available, out of stock, unavailable. Both screens now rank first.
- 2026-08-19: both screens are fixtures 15 and 16. Sixteen fixtures, fourteen first, sixteen in the top three, and no wrong ranking that stayed silent.
- 2026-08-19: a third pair, a medication reminder sheet, run by eye and through the skill. The eye caught eight things, the skill wrote five rows and one question, and the library asked one thing neither the eye nor the brief had reached: what a queued reminder does when the person crosses a time zone, which for a dose interval is a safety question rather than a polish question. Fixture 17. Seventeen fixtures, fifteen first, all seventeen in the top three.
- 2026-08-19: a real slip during a run wrote five rows while the reply claimed six, and the reply check passed it. Step 7 tells you to take counts from a command and nothing enforced that, so a count nobody ran read exactly like one that did. check_reply.py now reads the ledger named in the reply and compares both counts, and fails when the file is not there at all. Proved by feeding it a reply claiming nine rows and seven inherited against a ledger holding six and three.
- 2026-08-19: three more pairs, each screen read by eye and then through the skill: a launcher command palette with an actions panel, a report sheet on a video app, and a writing assistant panel that counts your defects and withholds the fixes. Trade words were added for keyboard surfaces, for community safety, and for persuasion, and all three now rank first.

Oficia Mono, a new monospaced typeface released 19 August 2026. Based on an early grotesk expanded
into a mono, one weight, light. Heart-dotted i's. Stylistically informed by Antique Olive and Gill
Sans; the designer wanted a face that fills the need for Gill Sans in order to overwrite Eric Gill.
Released with a specimen made with another designer, reimagined as a publication, performance,
installation and live print event, with writers contributing fictional performance reviews as the
specimen's content. The letterset is treated as a character with a personality, later the
protagonist of a novel. Also released as Oficia Poly, a bold companion.

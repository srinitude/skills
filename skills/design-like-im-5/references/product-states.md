# Product states

Parent and backlink: [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md), Discover product states.

Read this file before `state_judgment`. Use it again when proof shows a new state, change, clash, or old guess.

## State meaning

A product state tells what is true now. It can change what a person sees, knows, does, expects, or can fix.

Check the live view. Check the live act. Name what is true now. Keep each fact plain. Mark each gap. Show each link. Keep doubt in view.

States come from current proof. A label starts a check. It does not close the list.

One scene may hold many states. States may overlap, nest, conflict, alternate, fade, return, or affect one person.

A state may be unknown, mixed, old, brief, late, in doubt, part known, or guessed. Mark the base for each claim.

## Discovery frame

- **Person:** Check name, role, skill, words, age, access, trust, stress, tiredness, private needs, and what they know.
- **Goal:** Check need, worth, risk, undo, how often, time, shared owner, and proof of success.
- **Task:** Check start, progress, pause, resume, cancel, undo, retry, switch, leave, return, and done.
- **Prior act:** Check what was open, typed, picked, given, denied, changed, lost, saved, shared, or undone.
- **Data:** Check gaps, lag, age, clash, size, order, fit, owner, sync, source, and risk.
- **System:** Check ready, busy, capped, slow, off, failed, fixed, in line, rate cap, and part ready.
- **Place:** Check light, noise, motion, safety, privacy, crowds, weather, focus, link, and who is near.
- **Device:** Check size, pose, turn, power, heat, camera, sound, tools, keys, mouse, and shared use.
- **Input:** Check touch, mouse, keys, voice, switch, gaze, pen, remote, tool use, paste, scan, and access input.
- **Access:** Check sight, sound, motion, speech, thought, words, color, zoom, focus, time, and calm motion.
- **Time:** Check first use, repeat use, due time, timeout, wait, age, time zone, order, break, and return.
- **Content:** Check length, kind, words, tone, risk, trust, doubt, media, warnings, and lost parts.
- **People:** Check solo, shared, watched, sent out, seen, handed off, in doubt, approved, blocked, and same-time work.
- **Risk:** Check safety, money, privacy, rights, access, name, hard change, leaks, law, and repair cost.

Add any factor shown by the current product. Never treat this frame as the full set.

## State prompts

- **Content:** Check no content, first content, old content, partial content, conflicting content, hidden content, and too much content.
- **Progress:** Check idle, waiting, loading, queued, saving, syncing, processing, streaming, and delayed feedback.
- **Input:** Check valid input, invalid input, incomplete input, duplicate input, changed input, lost input, and unsafe input.
- **Permission:** Check unknown, asked for, denied, limited, expired, revoked, and restored.
- **Link:** Check strong, weak, unstable, offline, restored, and split across devices.
- **Action:** Check ready, blocked, busy, disabled, hard to undo, able to be undone, complete, repeated, and undone.
- **Error:** Check known, unknown, local, remote, part failed, repeated, fixed, and not fixable.
- **Break:** Check before action, during action, after action, during payment, during save, and during handoff.
- **Success:** Check shown, late, partial, disputed, able to be undone, shared, and tied to later work.
- **Old state:** Check stale view, data, rule, permission, session, proof, and linked part.

Skip a prompt only when current evidence shows it cannot apply. Save that evidence and reason.

## Relations and change

- **Scope:** State whether the state affects one item, view, flow, person, device, account, or service.
- **Cause:** Keep seen causes, possible causes, and planned causes apart.
- **Entry and exit:** Name entry signs, exit signs, and changes that may happen with no page change.
- **At once:** Name states that happen at once. Do not merge them when they need different replies.
- **Inside:** Name nested states. A local error may sit inside a working page, weak network, and expiring session.
- **Conflict:** Name conflicts. A saved form may still hold stale permission or a failed remote write.
- **Age:** Record when the proof was seen and what may make it stale.
- **Doubt:** Record what is unknown, why it matters, and the least proof that could clear it.

## Eye review for every state

- **First sight:** What pulls the eye, what fades, and what meaning shows before close reading?
- **Order:** Does the shown order match the task, cause, need, linked work, and safe act order?
- **Groups:** Do space, shape, edge, motion, and line show the right links?
- **Change:** Can a person see what entered, left, changed, remained, moved, or became stale?
- **Reading:** Are words complete, readable, specific, and close to the thing they explain?
- **Color:** Does meaning survive color loss, low contrast, bright light, dark mode, and altered vision?
- **Focus:** Is key or access focus shown, in order, steady, and tied to the live task?
- **Range:** Does the view work at narrow, wide, zoomed, enlarged, rotated, and cropped sizes?
- **Motion:** Does movement explain change without hiding content, causing sickness, or delaying control?
- **Content:** Are text, images, controls, status, and empty space kept apart and easy to read?

Add any sight factor that could change the choice. Record exact spots, sizes, frames, and times when seen.

## Brain review for every state

- **Truth:** Does the view match actual system, data, permission, cost, risk, and done state?
- **Next action:** Can the person tell what can happen now, what cannot, and why?
- **Cause:** Can the person connect the state to the act, event, rule, or failure that caused it?
- **Memory:** Must the person remember hidden facts, prior choices, codes, steps, positions, or short-lived content?
- **Choice:** Are options distinct, easy to compare, able to be undone where possible, and free from false rank?
- **Language:** Do words match the person's goal, product meaning, risk, and likely reading context?
- **Feedback:** Does each act get fast, clear, lasting, and well-placed feedback?
- **Risk:** Are harm, cost, privacy, hard change, and repair shown before the act?
- **Context:** Does the view preserve place, progress, scope, prior work, ownership, and linked effects?
- **Unknowns:** Does the product show doubt instead of false trust or a quiet fail?

Add any mind factor that could change the choice. Keep facts, views, choices, and doubts apart.

## Touch review for every state

- **Targets:** Are controls large, separate, stable, reachable, and clear under current input conditions?
- **Spacing:** Can a person act without touching a nearby, dangerous, or unrelated control?
- **Reach:** Do posture, hand, device, grip, and one-handed use change access to primary actions?
- **Input:** Does the product accept, keep, check, shape, and restore input without surprise?
- **Cues:** Does each control show its current action, state, and whether it can be used?
- **Response:** Is press, drag, key, voice, switch, or pointer feedback fast and tied to the action?
- **Gestures:** Is each one clear, not forced when needed, easy to undo, and free from hidden clashes?
- **Repair:** Can a person undo, retry, edit, resume, cancel, or recover without losing unrelated work?
- **Settings:** Does the state still follow zoom, text size, motion, sound, contrast, input, and platform settings?
- **Effort:** Does the state avoid extra steps, repeat input, long reach, fine aim, and body strain?

Add any body factor that could change the choice. Record input kind, act, reply, lag, and repair.

## Bad and failed cases

- Reject text that overlaps text.
- Reject images that cover needed images, text, status, or controls.
- Reject clipped meaning, off-view actions, covered controls, target collisions, focus traps, lost cues, and task dead ends.
- Reject a hidden state, false priority, false control, missing feedback, lost work, blocked access, or danger near routine action.
- Reject a design fixed to one context.
- Reject broken shared parts, shifted work, hidden cost, and hidden risk.
- Reject an ungrounded claim, unseen pass, missed state, closed state set, missed check, weak options, and script judgment.
- Reject stale proof, broken links, vague results, style-first work, surface copy, screen-first rules, and early choice.
- Reject one-sided proof, happy-path-only work, late access work, metric overreach, and polish that hides a failed task.

These are hard bars when named fail proof exists. The model still checks current proof and records the choice.

## Response exploration

- Create at least four unlike response directions for each kept state. More directions may be needed.
- Start with a known form, a product-shaped form, a true reverse, and a bold edge.
- Add, split, mix, or reverse directions when evidence suggests another useful response.
- Record the scene, claim, fit, evidence, tradeoffs, veto check, test, and novelty for each direction.
- Test each path with states at once, states inside states, doubt, old proof, breaks, size, access, and repair.
- Keep a bold direction until evidence or a fixed veto rejects it. Do not reject it because it is new.
- Compare current eye, brain, touch, harm, access, agency, and product proof before choosing.
- Keep choice and visible reason with the model. A script may require fields, IDs, paths, and answer coverage only.

## Record and stop

- Use [state-record.schema.json](../assets/state-record.schema.json) for the record shape. Use the current packet for required context paths.
- Record the open-world flag, states, links, changes, reply options, choice, proof, counterproof, doubt, and linked work.
- Record shown reasons and proof. Do not ask for or save private thought traces.
- Return `BLOCKED` for the affected claim when needed proof, sight, input, rights, access, or safe recovery is missing.
- Do not block unrelated safe work. Name the failed gate, affected states, safe work, and smallest proof needed to resume.

A passing schema proves only record shape. It does not prove the chosen state response is clear, useful, safe, or good.

A social platform completed its first major brand refresh in over ten years. Brighter, more vibrant
and more expressive, reflecting a shift from a photo sharing app into a platform for many kinds of
creation. The system has to work for three billion people across every market, in app icon, type,
colour, motion and sound. It has to celebrate everyone's point of view without becoming so flexible
that nothing reads as the brand. Legacy gradient recognition is an asset the refresh must not spend.

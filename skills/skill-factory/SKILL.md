## Outcome

Produce a complete, portable skill that accomplishes its declared result and proves that result under its actual conditions of use. Apply this contract to the factory and every skill it creates, updates, standardizes, imports, adapts, refreshes, invokes, checks or maintains. Include every instruction, reference, asset, schema, configuration, template, script, workflow, test, example, evaluation, integration and output. Preserve the intended purpose, accepted behavior, source bytes, authorized scope, independent variants, lineage and unrelated work. Keep all mandatory requirements conjunctive. A successful workflow, valid package or passing average cannot compensate for a missing capability or mandatory proof.

Read this entire body first. It is the central owner of core behavior, the reusable ledger method and everything needed to understand the first decision. Budget context without removing required full reads. On the first loading and every successful later loading, the invoking agent must load the entire current governing ledger, all always-required rules and relationships, and the declared initial resources before dependent work. Resolve their actual paths, identities, public routes and consumers through the [use-case record][use-case], checked by `mise run use-case-policy`. Missing, stale, misrouted or ignored required content blocks its consumer. A reference, digest, receipt, earlier loading or acknowledgment does not prove that the current content was read and used. The invoking agent owns integration and final proof after any authorized delegation.

Resolve availability and audience before affected writes. This factory has `metadata.scope: "user"`. Every new or changed skill has exactly one scope string: `user` means availability across the user's projects; `project` means availability for one project or repository. The use-case record owns `audience.primary: human` or `agent`, according to who consumes the main result. Preserve valid designations and other valid metadata unless an authorized change or resolved purpose conflict requires otherwise. Record secondary and output-specific consumers where they change the contract. Scope, executor, report recipient, working directory, registry location and generic wording cannot substitute for the primary audience. Invalid values, types and duplicate keys fail. Untouched legacy inspection may allow missing designations; changed-output acceptance may not. Use `mise run resolve-scope` and the [scope contract](references/skill-scope-contract.md) for unresolved scope, then the supported host integration for authorized placement. Classification grants no installation, movement, duplication or permission authority.

A human-facing report does not change an agent primary result. An audience change adapts inputs, instructions, interfaces, presentation, evals and human participation; relabeling is insufficient. |

| Writing and acting | The authorized model or program writes the stated artifact; the effect owner performs the real operation. Mere planning, generated commands, process startup or a successful exit does not prove the intended effect. |
| Judging | Models retain meaning, relevance, causal reasoning, perception and creative judgment. Humans retain their authentic decisions and assigned evidence. Neither a schema nor a workflow makes these judgments deterministic. |
| Verifying and validating | Verification checks a stated property with a capable predicate or evaluator. Validation determines whether the integrated result serves the intended need in its actual context. Name the responsible acceptance owner and limits. |
| Proof and handoff | Bind the claim to actual inputs, selected outputs, versions, authority, evidence contents and responsible judgment. Pass the necessary context and truthful status to the next owner; delivery alone is not consumption or acceptance. |

## Motivation

The factory exists to turn intent into usable capability while making the reasons, actions and proof inspectable. Separate repeatable mechanics from substantive judgment so automation removes avoidable work without narrowing intelligence, creativity, craftsmanship, taste or available authorized modalities. Give every material requirement its protected outcome and the failure it prevents. Keep conditions and valid alternatives so an executor can adapt without defeating the purpose. A technically correct component can still be wrong for its task, recipient or integration.

Keep one canonical meaning owner, stable term, default route and material decision per branch. Reuse accepted structures before adding code or dependencies. Remove demonstrated duplication, decorative sections, needless indirection and choices with no outcome difference. Simple means understandable and complete under the contract. Line counts, task counts, dependency counts, token savings and speed never justify lost meaning, context, safety, privacy, accessibility, authority, proof or domain complexity. Measure the whole accepted result, including retries, waits and recipient effort, rather than rewarding isolated activity. Choose algorithms, data structures, formats, batching and concurrency from measured access, mutation, queries, order, interoperability and lifetime; reuse native streaming, indexes and atomic replacement where their actual guarantees fit.

Prevent WorkSlop: plausible output that fails its task or transfers unfinished reasoning, checking or repair to its recipient. Establish the real need and next action, complete the producer's work, verify claims and sources, make the result usable, and expose actual limits. More content, attractive presentation, AI adoption, subjective satisfaction or technically sound code do not establish usefulness. Read the relevant original [WorkSlop research](https://www.betterup.com/workslop), [preprint](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/5f78h_v1) and [survey](https://zapier.com/blog/ai-workslop/) through the research route in `mise run domain-research-policy`; retain their methods, populations and uncertainty. Consider the actual conditions, culture, agency and meaningful purpose of the work. Do not turn associations, commercial surveys or research examples into universal causal claims.

## Mise task graph

Mise owns the public command surface, pinned environment and outer prerequisites. Mastra owns each delegated domain workflow and its supported control/state/recovery. Tested scripts perform repeatable leaves and exact predicates directly in that environment. Caller-controlled models own residual meaning, causal reasoning, research interpretation, perception and creative capability. Humans supply their assigned authentic evidence and decisions; the host owns identity, permission and supported communication. Inventory the actual actors, tools, external systems and governed resources before assigning duties. Keep one owner per scheduling, retry, cache, state, cleanup and protected effect. Put an explicit handoff between these owners; never recursively invoke competing Mise graphs from workflow steps. A mandatory Mastra workflow must execute the skill's actual capability, including the factory's own domain work, rather than wrapping bookkeeping or a generic proxy.

## Steps

1. **Resolve the contract.** Model: read this whole body and current initial inputs; identify command, outcome, purpose, audience, scope, accepted behavior, fixed constraints, mandatory methods and sufficient proof. Branch: `help` returns the grammar; `doctor` returns its supported readiness result; otherwise continue the matching operation. Stop: pause affected work for an unmatched command, unretrievable material fact or actual authority gate; ask one concise question and keep eligible independent work moving.
2. **Establish usable prerequisites.** Model: inspect the real environment, owners/callers, tool interfaces, complete package/source inventory and dependencies. Mise: run `mise run doctor` and relevant policy routes through their owning scripts. Readback: actual readiness, version/configuration/resource bindings, authorized effects, source integrity and preserved baseline. If: a required source is inaccessible or identity differs, report expected/observed identity and stop its consumer without normalizing the source or silently accepting another baseline. Explore safely under provisional inputs only where fixed effect/acceptance gates allow it.
3. **Reconcile the governing ledger.** Model: use `mise run ledger` and its actual Mastra read/review workflow to read every authorized governing document and the entire current ledger. Reconcile source-to-record and record-to-source coverage, file roles, facets, typed relationships, applicability and domain use. 4. **Freeze the executable change plan.** Model: select the smallest capable current owner, resolve the public command and prepare a versioned plan with all ordered steps, owners, inputs, resource arguments, effects, observable readbacks, failure/recovery, tests and propagation. 5. **Implement the selected operation.** Mastra: execute the domain plan with existing scripts and authorized runners, preserving model capabilities and caller authority.

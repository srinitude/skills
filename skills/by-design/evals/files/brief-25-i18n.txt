Localisation readiness pass on a shopping app before it launches in new markets. English strings are padded by about thirty percent during the sprint to expose layouts that cannot take German or Finnish, while Japanese runs shorter and hides the same defects. Right to left layouts for Arabic and Hebrew have to be checked on real devices rather than in a spreadsheet, since mirroring decisions are made per element. Dates, decimals and currency formats break at the checkout step where they matter most. Clipped buttons and overflowing labels turn up under pseudo locale testing, and store screenshots, push notifications and empty states all need the same review as the interface itself.

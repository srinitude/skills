# Evidence base

This ledger supports the method. It does not provide a target population's base rate. Recheck every load-bearing claim and magnitude at the primary source before use.

## Use rules

- Keep each result tied to its population, setting, outcome, design, and uncertainty.
- Mark `Direct` when the source supports the wording and `Inference` when the application extends beyond it.
- Require two independent author teams or datasets for every load-bearing premise.
- Never turn a correlation, effect size, coefficient, or single field result into a universal conversion rule.
- Framework sources explain constructs. They do not supply a probability for a new behavior.

## Intent and action

Reviews find that intention and behavior are related but not interchangeable, and experimental changes in intention can exceed changes in observed behavior. The gap varies by behavior, follow-up, and measurement method. Use stated intent as a directional signal, not a fixed completion rate.

- [Intent and behavior review](https://cdr.lib.unc.edu/downloads/9w032f333?locale=en)
- [Experimental intention and behavior meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16536643/)
- [Prospective health-behavior review](https://researchers.mq.edu.au/en/publications/prospective-prediction-of-health-related-behaviours-with-the-theo)

## Hypothetical and observed commitments

Hypothetical willingness can exceed observed behavior, but study design and mitigation explain some variation. A domain-specific field gap cannot be transported as a universal divisor. Prefer consequential prices, refundable reservations, real offers, or observed completion.

- [Hypothetical-bias meta-analysis](https://repository.lsu.edu/ag_econ_pubs/150/)
- [Stated and actual donation-frequency study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33690931/)

## Self-report and social desirability

Question sensitivity, privacy, mode, wording, and perceived norms can bias self-report. Domain reviews also find small and heterogeneous associations in some settings. Treat social desirability as a possible mechanism, not an automatic percentage correction.

- [Social-desirability review](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11135-011-9640-9)
- [Environmental self-report meta-analyses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7393925/)

## Habit and implementation

Stable cues and repetition can support automaticity, but formation time varies widely and study quality is uneven. If-then plans can help enact an existing goal, but they do not prove demand or create a valued goal. Measure cue stability, repetition, automaticity, and retention locally.

- [Habit theory review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17907866/)
- [Health-habit formation review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11641623/)
- [Implementation-intention review](https://cancercontrol.cancer.gov/sites/default/files/2020-06/goal_intent_attain.pdf)
- [Physical-activity review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6235272/)

## Switching and loss

Loss sensitivity varies by task and method. Ownership, migration work, risk, and switching cost should be tested in the target setting. No fixed multiplier is a product-adoption law.

- [Broad loss-aversion meta-analysis](https://ideas.repec.org/a/aea/jeclit/v62y2024i2p485-516.html)
- [Risky-choice loss-aversion meta-analysis](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/185745/13/1-s2.0-S0167487024000485-main.pdf)

## Population and outside-view limits

Samples can be unusual on behavioral measures, and researchers should state the populations, materials, settings, and historical conditions to which a result may apply. A reference class needs explicit inclusion rules and the same outcome and window as `[x]`.

- [Population-sampling critique](https://www2.psych.ubc.ca/~henrich/pdfs/WeirdPeople.pdf)
- [Constraints on generality](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28853993/)
- [Outside-view forecasting paper](https://bear.warrington.ufl.edu/brenner/mar7588/Papers/kahneman-lovallo-mansci1993.pdf)

## Excluded rules

Do not use startup postmortem percentages, one hypothetical-to-real divisor, one prosocial gap, a universal loss multiplier, a fixed switching threshold, a universal habit timeline, or friction described as the single top cause.

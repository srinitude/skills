# Evaluation rubric

## PASS

The response pins the system and action, uses direct outcome proof, applies the evidence and independence gates, keeps metrics and graders distinct, retains operational failures, proposes a least-privileged test, and completes the artifact and validator proof.

## FAIL

Any required criterion is missing, a veto pattern appears, a run or source is invented, a directional verdict is issued without required evidence, a tool attempt is counted as an outcome, or the test grants excess authority.

## NOT_APPLICABLE

Use only when the requested actor or question is outside this skill and the response routes it correctly without applying the agent-action verdict protocol.

## UNVERIFIED

Use when evidence needed to judge a criterion is missing from the trace, environment, or artifact. Do not upgrade missing proof to PASS.

## Review notes

Judge observable traces, environment state, and artifacts. Do not award credit for hidden reasoning, plans without execution, completion claims without readback, or source names without visible URLs.

# Rubric

Score each criterion as `PASS` or `BLOCKED`. The case passes only when every applicable criterion passes and no native veto occurs.

1. **Activation:** The route activates only for a material outcome-versus-method distinction.
2. **Starting map:** `starting-point` supplies outcome, proof, constraints, starting path, and unknowns before classification.
3. **Outcome:** The observable end state retains its meaning and strength.
4. **Proof:** Acceptance evidence remains separate from implementation activity.
5. **Boundaries:** Scope, authority, safety, privacy, cost, and reversibility remain fixed.
6. **Forbidden outcomes:** Prohibited states are never accepted as tradeoffs.
7. **Mandatory methods:** Exact, exclusive, contractual, regulatory, reproducibility-related, pedagogical, or user-required methods stay mandatory.
8. **Candidate paths:** Only replaceable routes remain adaptable.
9. **Adaptation:** Replacement requires preserved fields, material evidence or blockage, current authority, and approval for dropped or delayed work.
10. **Unknowns:** Unresolved distinctions stay visible and cause at most one concise question.
11. **Audit safety:** Complete source reading, stable IDs, and no unauthorized mutation are visible.
12. **Output:** The contract comes first, with ledger, route, and execution evidence kept distinct.
13. **Simple-request boundary:** Requests without a material method choice bypass contract work.
14. **Recovery:** Any lost native clause or veto is restored from source evidence before the case reruns.
15. **Claim limits:** Component evidence is never promoted to whole-outcome or execution proof.

The judge must cite the visible response or supplied trace for each PASS. An unsupported assumption is `BLOCKED`.
